## What I learned

- Time-based splits require sorted data before feature engineering to avoid leakage from future information
- sklearn pipelines with `Pipeline` + `StandardScaler` + `IsolationForest` require careful handling of `feature_cols_in_` during inference to avoid feature mismatch
- Plotly's `px.histogram` with string-categorical bins needs explicit casting to avoid warnings
